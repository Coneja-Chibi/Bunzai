Contrast check

Primary text needs 7:1 and every other text role 4.5:1 on every surface it sits on. Control boundaries, focus and meter fills need 3:1. A failing pair says fail in the last column; color is never the only signal.

Semantic roles: one jewel, one meaning

Every UI color that means something reads one of these seven roles, never a bare hex. brand is rose: the logo and title moments only, never a UI state. action is sapphire: primary buttons, links, focus. select is amethyst: selected, current, active, clearly apart from action. ok emerald, warn topaz, danger ruby are status. info aquamarine is HUD readouts, meters and data, and the default tint. Each has a fill (under dark ink), a text/mark shade, and a deep fill (under white).

Elevation and layers

Flat by default: framed, scrimmed or dropped by a hard edge, never a blurred glow. Every z-index comes from this list; 1000 and up is the host's protected layer.

Motion

Motion says something changed or something is waiting. No shake, jitter, glitch or flicker. Reduced motion is checking; under it every duration drops to zero and nothing lifts.

Focus and accessibility

Press Tab. The ring is an outline in --bk-focus-color, offset from the edge, so a frame, a bracket or an offset shadow can never cover it.
