Workbench

Autosave

Save half a second after you stop typing. Ctrl+S still works, and still saves at once.

Reopen where you left off

Restore the pieces you had open, and which one you were editing, after a restart or a crash.

Editor layout

Bento shows every field at once; Playbill turns them into acts you page through.

How you fill it in

Grid shows every field to edit directly; Steps walks you through like a quiz.

After sending a piece

Follow it to the Workbench, stay where you are, or ask every time.

Remote access

Turn on remote access

Bunzai stays on your machine. A private, encrypted link lets your own devices reach it, with no router changes and no ports opened. You sign in to Tailscale once (free); we open that page for you.

Local network access

A no-account option: reach this studio from devices on the same network, with no Tailscale sign-in. Each device enters a connect code, and you approve it here before it gets in.

Settings

Carried / New: Carried in full: Appearance (theme and house-accent swatches), Models (list, add/edit form, key-never-returned note), Connections (MCP list with a rejected-server message, add/edit form, the Bunzai-as-server snippet), Studio (home app, Library first deck, publish targets, engine checkouts), Workbench (autosave, reopen, editor layout and mode, follow behavior), About (update check, studio folder, project links, Windows shortcuts, Restart/Quit/Reset with the armed confirm state). Security, Remote access and Updates each have many more states in their own soft wireframes (10, 11 and 14 respectively, mostly dialogs and connection phases) than BUILD-BRIEF’s row names individually; this page carries one representative pair of states per area (Security: on/off; Remote: off/connected; Updates: ready/progress) rather than the full dialog set, to keep the page buildable in this pass. Every remaining Security/Remote/Updates state (the recovery-word dialogs, Tailscale phases, rollback confirms, etc.) is NOT yet drawn here; flagging this gap rather than silently shipping partial coverage. Secrets and recovery words are protected.* and are not drawn on this page (none of the carried Security states reach a secret-revealing dialog). Phone width is covered by the page-wide Width switch, not repeated here.
