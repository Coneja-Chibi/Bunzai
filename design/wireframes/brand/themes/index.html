THEME 1 · YOUR IDEA

The Call Board

The corkboard by the stage door where everything gets pinned: sticky notes, the cast list, the sign-in sheet, flyers.

How the site works

  • The home page IS the board. Every section is a note you can pick up.
  • The cast list is the feature tour; the sign-in sheet is the download.
  • Guides are a rehearsal-report binder hanging on a nail. Releases are dated call sheets.

Signature moves

  • Drag notes around; they stay where you left them.
  • Click a note to unpin it and it opens full-size.
  • New releases appear as a fresh note with a pin dropping in.
RoleCall DNA: the theater world and the cast list. Its own: cork, paper, handwriting, pins.

THEME 2

The Dressing Room Mirror

A vanity mirror ringed with warm bulbs, with photos, tickets and notes tucked into the frame. Old Hollywood, but make it cute.

How the site works

  • The home page is the mirror. Things tucked in the frame are the sections.
  • The mirror itself shows the live chat demo, like the bunny practicing lines.
  • Each page is a different drawer of the vanity (makeup box = presets, script = guides).

Signature moves

  • The bulbs warm up as the page loads.
  • Pull a photo out of the frame to open it.
  • Lipstick notes written on the glass as headings.
RoleCall DNA: marquee bulbs, the stage. Its own: intimate and personal instead of grand.

THEME 3

The Scrapbook

Someone's personal theater scrapbook: polaroids, washi tape, stickers, and handwriting in the margins. The "notes app chic" and scrapbook trends of 2026.

How the site works

  • The site is a book. Pages turn left and right instead of scrolling.
  • Features are spreads, guides are how-to pages with doodled arrows, releases are dated diary entries.
  • The table of contents is the first spread.

Signature moves

  • Page-turn transitions.
  • Stickers you can peel and restick.
  • Handwritten notes animate as if being written.
RoleCall DNA: keepsakes of shows. Its own: diary-personal, soft, scribbly.

THEME 4

The Riso Program

A risograph-printed show program: two or three bright inks (fluoro pink, blue, yellow), slightly misregistered, with a grainy print texture.

How the site works

  • The site is a folded program; each page is a numbered spread.
  • A running order (Act I, II, III) is the navigation, like a program's contents.
  • Guides are the "notes from the director" pages; releases are the "cast changes" insert.

Signature moves

  • Ink layers slide into register as you scroll.
  • Every page uses only its two inks.
  • A tear-off coupon for the download.
RoleCall DNA: the playbill. Its own: indie, loud, printed-by-hand color.

THEME 5

The Sticker Sheet

"Cute-alism": plain, raw layout, but everything clickable is a glossy die-cut sticker with a white border. Stationery shop meets app.

How the site works

  • Each page is a sticker sheet; the stickers are the content.
  • Peeling a sticker (click and drag) opens it; the backing paper underneath shows the details.
  • Your peeled stickers collect in a little tray, like a history.

Signature moves

  • Real peel animation with a curling corner.
  • Stickers wobble on hover.
  • The download is the big round sticker you peel off.
RoleCall DNA: the bold outline and rose. Its own: glossy, bouncy, toy-like.

THEME 6

The Prop Table

The backstage prop table, seen from above: every prop sits on its taped outline with a masking-tape label.

How the site works

  • The home page is the table. Each prop is a section: the script is Guides, the ticket is Download, the lamp lights up the live demo.
  • Picking up a prop zooms into it; putting it down goes back.
  • Missing props (not built yet) leave just the taped outline, "coming soon".

Signature moves

  • Props lift and cast a shadow when hovered.
  • Masking-tape labels as all the navigation.
  • The lamp is a real toggle: switch it on to start the demo scene.
RoleCall DNA: stagecraft. Its own: tactile, object-based, playful.
