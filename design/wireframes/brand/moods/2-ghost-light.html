01The idea

The Ghost Light

someone always leaves one light on

When a theater closes for the night, a single bare bulb on a stand is left burning center stage. Superstition says it's for the ghosts; stage managers say it keeps you from falling in the pit. Either way, it's the most romantic object in theater.

RoleCall is the show at full brightness. Bunraku is the same stage at 2am, when it's just you, the bulb, and whatever you want to rehearse. Private, a little spooky, completely yours.

The site is dark on purpose. You explore it with a light. Features are spike marks taped on the floor, glowing in fluorescent tape colors, where each thing "stands" on the stage.
