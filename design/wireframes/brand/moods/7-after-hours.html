01The idea

After Hours

The house is dark. Your room is still lit.

The show is over and everyone went home. Two lights are left on in the building: the ghost light on the empty stage, and the mirror bulbs in your dressing room down the hall.

RoleCall is the show at full brightness. Bunraku is the same theater after close: the stage is yours to rehearse on, and the room is yours to live in. Dark, warm, a little spooky, completely private.

Two places, two jobs. The stage is the public part: tour, guides, download, marked with glow tape and found with your light. The dressing room is the personal part: your library, your characters and your notes, tucked into the mirror frame.
