01The idea

The Dressing Room

your room is ready, darling

Past the stage door, down a narrow hall, there's a room with your name on it. A mirror ringed with bulbs, photos and good-luck cards tucked into the frame, lipstick notes on the glass, a telegram from someone who couldn't make it.

RoleCall is the stage everyone sees. Bunraku is the room where you become the character: private, glamorous in a worn-in way, full of your things. It's the "home away from home" that performers personalize.

The site is warm, oxblood and gold, and intimate. Every page is a different corner of your room. Nothing here is for the audience.
