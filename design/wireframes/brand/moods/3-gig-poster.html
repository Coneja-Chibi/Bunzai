01The idea

The Gig Poster

one night only. every night.

Before the big venue there's the basement show: a screenprinted poster wheatpasted to a wall, two inks that don't quite line up, a lineup in huge letters, tear-off tabs at the bottom so you can take the details home.

RoleCall is the opera house with a season brochure. Bunzai is the DIY show flyer: loud, cheap-looking on purpose, made by people who care more about the show than the venue.

Every page is a poster. The lineup is the feature list. Releases are tour dates. The download is a tab you literally tear off.
