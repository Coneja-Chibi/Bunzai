01The idea

The Call Board

sign in, you're in the show

Every theater has one: the corkboard by the stage door. It holds the cast list, the sign-in sheet, rehearsal reports, a dozen sticky notes and at least one sign nobody reads. It is the most honest object in the building: the real show, before the polish.

RoleCall is the house: velvet, gold and a curtain. Bunzai is the wall behind it, painted black, with a clip light and a board full of paper. The whole site is that board. You pull things off it to read them and pin them back when you're done.

It feels like a company, not a product: people wrote on this, it changes every week, and your name can go on the sheet.
