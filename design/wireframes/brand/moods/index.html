Six backstage identities

RoleCall is the house. Bunraku lives somewhere behind it. Each board is a full identity: idea, palette, type, materials, interface pieces, site structure, moves and voice.

  1. 01The Call BoardCork, paper, post-its, the sign-in sheet. Sign in, you're in the show.
  2. 02The Ghost LightThe empty stage at 2am, one bulb, glow tape. You explore with a light.
  3. 03The Gig PosterTwo fluoro inks on black stock, tear-off tabs. One night only, every night.
  4. 04The BoothBlue running lights, the prompt book, cue lamps. Standby, go.
  5. 05The Dressing RoomOxblood, brass, lipstick on the mirror. Your room is ready, darling.
  6. 07After HoursGhost Light + Dressing Room. The house is dark. Your room is still lit.
  7. 06The Road CaseStenciled touring cases, gaff tape, luggage tags. Load in, set up, play.
