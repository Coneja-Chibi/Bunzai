Notes · how this one works

No theme. Cute from color and type, theater only in the corners.

night
card
pink
peach
butter
mint
sky

What makes it soft

  • Dusky plum dark, never black
  • Five pastel accents, one per feature
  • A soft, slightly wonky serif (Fraunces)
  • Slow, small motion: a bobbing bun, a lifting card
  • Lots of breathing room

The theater nods

  • Sections are "acts"
  • Features are "the cast", with dotted leaders
  • Download is a notched ticket with a tear line
  • One row of marquee dots
  • The ticket bun logo

Kept out on purpose

  • Rounded corners (only dots and the logo are round)
  • Gradients, glows, glass
  • Script fonts
  • Emoji and sparkle icons
  • Big dark-and-red drama
