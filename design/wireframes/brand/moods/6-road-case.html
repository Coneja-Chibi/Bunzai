01The idea

The Road Case

load in. set up. play.

Touring shows live in road cases: black, aluminum-edged, stenciled with the show name, patched with gaff tape and marker labels, luggage tags zip-tied to the handles. They go everywhere, take abuse and hold everything the show needs.

RoleCall is the resident company in its own theater. Bunzai is the touring production: it comes to your computer, packed tight, nothing left at the venue. Private because it's yours, sturdy because it travels.

The site is a loading dock. Every section is a case you unlatch; the foam cutouts inside show exactly what's packed.
