    01The idea

    The Booth

    Standby cue 1. Cue 1, go.

    Every show is run from a dark booth at the back. The stage manager sits under blue running lights (dim enough not to spill into the house) with the prompt book open, pencil cue marks down the margin, and calls the whole show on headset: "Standby. Go."

    RoleCall is what the audience sees. Bunraku is the person running it: the one who knows every cue, controls every light and makes the magic happen on time.

    The site is calm, precise and blue. You move through it by calling cues. The script is the guide. The GO button is the most satisfying button on the internet.
