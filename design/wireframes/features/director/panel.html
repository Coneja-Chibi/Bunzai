Story Director panel

The right wing "story-director", opened from the Story Director row in the Props wing (gear) or from the floating activity button. Five tabs as RoleCall draws them: Story, Arcs, Analysis, Activity, Debug. RoleCall's copy is kept word for word. Three deliberate differences are marked: arc cards now show the planned next beat, the activity log survives a reload, and every edit saves to the current branch tip so a swipe or a branch switch never leaves the panel showing the wrong story.

What the panel reads. The Director state saved with the newest reply on the branch you are looking at. Turn history (Latest, 3 / 14) steps back through earlier replies' snapshots; stepping back is read only, and every edit button is off until you are on Latest. What it writes. Only the person's own edits (Mark as Complete, delete a seed, arc or note, add a hidden arc or note, Reset, Clear activity). Nothing here is ever hard deleted: a removed item leaves the panel and the prompt and stays in the saved state.
