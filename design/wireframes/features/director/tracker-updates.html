Story Director: tracker updates

Addendum to the Director wireframes (plan 16). After a reply is saved, the Director updates the trackers that are due, in the background. Every part below is RoleCall's own component, drawn with the app's real Director, Props and tracker styles. New against the shipped Director UI: Default AI Awareness and Max Tool Rounds (the two settings that were held back), a tracker's "DM Updates" row, the "Updated by the Story Director" line with Undo, and Tracker lines in the Activity tab.

Two numbers worth reading twice. Default AI Awareness is how many of your messages pass between updates (1 = after every reply, 0 = never by itself, up to 99). RoleCall's own screen says 0 but its worker uses 1; this build shows the real value, 1. Max Tool Rounds is the most model calls the Director spends on one turn, planning included (1 to 6, default 2). At 1 it can only plan, so no tracker is updated; the row says so. RoleCall's range (1 to 20 in the Booth) and its 4 are not copied: its control does nothing there.

Default AI Awareness

Controls how often enabled trackers ask Story Director to refresh state.

  • 0 means manual only.
  • 1 means every user turn.
  • Higher numbers reduce token use but update less often.
Max Tool Rounds

Limits how many model calls Story Director can spend on one turn, planning included.

  • Higher values can fix complex state, but cost more tokens and latency.
  • At 1 the Director only plans; trackers are not updated.

Behavior. The line shows the newest automatic update of that tracker and stays until you edit the tracker, refresh it, or a newer reply replaces it. Undo takes back only what the update changed, and leaves anything you changed since (the left-alone rule loadouts use), and says so. An update that was dropped (you edited while it was reading, or the answer could not be read) shows nothing in the frame: it is a line in Activity. The shipped frame's own Undo for your edits and Refresh keeps its place; this line never covers it.
