Tracker refresh

Two ways to re-read the chat into a tracker: the Refresh button on a tracker's own strip (one module), and Refresh Enabled Boards in the Props wing (every enabled module, one after another). RoleCall applies the answer straight away, even when the model's answer was a guess or empty. Here the answer is a proposal: you see what would be added, changed and removed, and nothing changes until you Accept. Accept saves with Undo.

Where the words come from. Button tooltip "Refresh from chat history" and the batch label "Refresh Enabled Boards" with "Refreshing {label} (n/m)" are RoleCall's. The visible "Refresh" label on the strip button, the review sheets and every failure line are new, because RoleCall shows nothing at all when a refresh fails. A refresh reads the last 20 messages plus the first message, and sees the tracker as it is now, so it updates rather than starts over. Removed rows are hidden and kept (never hard deleted) and come back with Undo.
