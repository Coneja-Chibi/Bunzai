Messenger shell, in Discord's architecture

Rail of rounded character spaces with folders, a channel-style sidebar (scenes as channels, takes as threads, studio pages as a category) with the persona panel pinned under it, the conversation column (header, grouped messages with replies and picture embeds, hover action bar, rounded composer) and a member list on the right. Colors come from Bunzai tokens: the window gradient runs from the selection purple through the rose-deep to the premiere rose, behind translucent token panels. Every frame is live: hover a message, right-click it, press "+" or the people icon.

Composer states (cropped)

Composer states. The steering strip sits on top of the pill like Discord's "Replying to" bar (today: Steering Vera's last reply, with Cancel; Send becomes Regenerate). With no connection the pill is dimmed and keeps the Open AI wing link (opens the AI page of the settings layer). While a reply streams, send is the Stop button.
