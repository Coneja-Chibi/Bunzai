Messenger settings: three scopes, one long page per category

One floating panel, opened from three places, each showing only what you opened it on. Select a category and its sections appear under it in the nav; the page is every section of that category stacked in one scrolling column, and scrolling highlights the current section in the nav. Rows with a chevron open a deeper page with a back arrow. Replaces today's single "settings workspace" with 16 or more flat programs (messenger-chrome.tsx, programs.ts). Click a scope button or a gear; scroll; click a sub-item; Esc or X closes.

Decision: scope follows ownership. Appearance and your AI connection belong to you; a lorebook, wardrobe or card belongs to the character; a preset, samplers, summary or tracker belongs to the scene. Today they share one flat list as if they were alike. Three windows, three entry points, one layout.

Open it from

Entry points. The dashed outline in the frame marks where the open window came from. User: the settings gear in the user panel (the same gear that opens today's workspace). Character: the character name dropdown atop the sidebar; the rail icon is the character, like a Discord server. Scene: a gear that shows on hover on each scene row, like a channel's gear.

Left column. User settings: your round avatar, name and "Edit profile" with a pencil. Character and Scene settings show that character's or scene's art and name with their own edit link. Then the search box, an unheaded first group, and small dim headers after thin dividers. Every category row has an icon; the selected one is filled. Under the selected category its sections appear indented along a thin vertical line; the current one has a short bar and brighter text. Other categories stay collapsed.

The page. Top bar: the category name at left, X at right, nothing else. Below, a centered column about 44rem wide, each section under a large heading, separated by a full-width divider and generous space. Rows put the label at left and, at right, a value then an Edit button, or a value then a chevron (opens a deeper page). Switch rows keep a title, a description and the switch at the right end. Drawn controls: paired dropdowns with labels above (Samplers, Appearance), sliders under their labels, radio groups with a title and a one-line description per option (Appearance, Control Booth), a primary button (Memory). Esc closes the window; Backspace goes up one deeper page.

Deeper pages. Only chevron rows open one: User > AI connection > Models; Character > Lorebooks > Canal District > The pump room; Scene > Presets and prompts > Narrator bank. The deeper page puts a back arrow and the parent section's name in the top bar, then its own large heading.

Search. Searches rows of the open scope and lists the matches with their category and section; here it dims nav rows that do not match. Per-page search boxes (guides, models, sampler parameters) stay for long lists. Corners. The theme has no radius token; this page uses one local value (10px, 8px on small controls) to show the shape, and the build needs one token for it.

Narrow state (Scene settings, nav screen)

Narrow widths (below about 900 px). The panel fills the screen and the first screen is the category list alone. Tapping a category opens its page with a back arrow to the list; sections become a horizontal chip row under the top bar in place of the nav sub-list, and scroll-spy keeps the current chip in view. Value-and-button rows wrap the button under the label; paired dropdowns stack. Matches today's tab view on narrow screens.
