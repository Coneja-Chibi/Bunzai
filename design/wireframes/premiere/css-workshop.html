This is the CSS Workshop 15

Two modes: Simple (guided ladder) and Advanced (code plus rule breakdown). Flip the Simple / Advanced toggle anytime.

  1. 1 Status strip: same top spot. Mono bar becomes a quiet UI-font line with a gold pulse mark; Reload Studio restyled as the quiet button at its right end.
  2. 2 Room header, same place and order (title block left, actions right): gold kicker "App · style" (was a mono caps eyebrow), Cinzel title with the violet glow (was heavy sans), lede in the UI font with a rose rule (was italic). Rule under it in gold.
  3. 3 Header actions, same row: Copy CSS (primary rose), Download .css, Clear draft, Tour, then the flash note. Import .css and the Simple / Advanced toggle are removed from the header; only the toolbar copies in the editor remain (callout 5).
  4. 4 The framed stage becomes one see-through Premiere panel (hard offset shadow, butter regex edge on top). Its dashed banner becomes a staged notice: gold left edge, Cinzel lead "CSS workshop.", UI-font prose.
  5. 5 Target row, same order: Targets, kit select, Mode (kit segmented: rose underline, gold hover), Import .css, Undo (only with history), flash note. Labels are 13px UI font, not mono caps. The native select is a kit select.
  6. 6 Ladder tabs (Simple only), same spot above the preview: IDE-style mono tabs become the chooser's spaced segmented tabs with a gold act number; the active one gets the rose edge and offset shadow; hover lifts.
  7. 7 Sealed preview, same place and size: framed well with a gold caption tab and a violet glow behind the mock card. Still a sandboxed iframe in the build.
  8. 8 Pane head: today's pane has none; a Cinzel pane title with a gold rule is added in the spot above its hint (spacing only). Hint text is UI font. Add-rule row keeps its order (label, select, + Blank rule).
  9. 9 Knobs stay a single column in today's order (Selector, Background, Text color, Type, Box, Opacity, Visibility, !important). Fields are kit inputs with gold focus ring; values stay mono since they are CSS; Solid / Gradient is the kit segmented control; opacity slider glows rose; !important is the kit switch.
  10. 10 Rule rows and the plain-English line: real hover (slide, gold edge, violet glow) and selected (thick rose edge, offset shadow, rose glow) states instead of flat lines. Delete rule stays the only danger button in the pane.
  11. 11 Starters: recipe rows become chooser-style cards (3px frame, butter top edge, offset shadow, lift and glow on hover); title is Cinzel, Use this is the button family.
  12. 12 Source and Advanced code: snippet chips are kit chips (gold code text, lift on hover); the editor stays mono with a line gutter, gold focus frame, and gold, violet and rose syntax tints.
  13. 13 Advanced keeps today's code-left, breakdown-right split (about 1.15 to .95) with its hint above. Counts are tabular numerals. Freeform stays a verbatim mono block with a violet edge. The empty and no-selection messages become a staged, centered Cinzel moment with a lede instead of a grey sentence.
  14. 14 Clear the draft dialog: a Premiere panel with a Cinzel title; Clear is the danger button, Keep is quiet.
  15. 15 Tour card: the same panel grammar (pink pack edge, Cinzel step title, segmented progress dots, Next as primary rose). Back disabled on step one.
  16. Simple Starters and Source, Advanced and the dialogs are stacked below the Assist stage only so every state shows; in the app they are the same stage switched by the Mode toggle and the ladder tabs, as today.
