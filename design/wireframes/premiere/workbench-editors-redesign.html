Harbour Lore
  1. Tab strip and Reload studio. The shell's tab strip gets the Premiere skin: deck-colored top edges for each kind, the active tab lifted onto the panel color. The status line and Reload studio sit at its right end instead of floating in the corners.
  2. Editor header. One framed header for every editor: kind monogram, name in Cinzel, one meta line with numbers in mono, write-for strip, save state, Desk and Nodes, focus. The mono caps labels (PRESET, SAVED, DESK) become sentence case in the UI font.
  3. Same panes as today. Preset: collapsible Identity and settings, prompt list on the left, Edit prompt, Live build and Preview rail on the right. Regex: rules list, rule page, Quick try rail. Lorebook: contents, entry page, live tools rail. Nothing moved, nothing hidden.
  4. Skin only. Panels, rose primary, gold for markers and help, deck edge for the piece kind. The mint switches and pastel tags are gone. Fields and selects are drawn with the kit, no native controls. The regex test bench, lorebook Cards and Web views, Book rules, and the Health and Changes panes keep their places as buttons and tabs and are not redrawn here.
