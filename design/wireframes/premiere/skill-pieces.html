  1. 1 The approved piece-editor header, on the lorebook's color: monogram, name, one meta line (when it turns on, where it goes, where it is linked), save state.
  2. 2 "What it is for" is the one line the AI reads when it may pick the skill, and the line the Library card and the scene show.
  3. 3 Instructions are plain prompt text with macros, like a preset block, with the same macro chips.
  4. 4 When it turns on: Always; When the chat mentions (keywords or a pattern over the last N messages, any or all, case and whole words), checked with the lorebook's matcher; When the AI picks it (opened with open_skill, stays open until the AI, you, or the close rule closes it). Click the segments.
  5. 5 Where it goes: with the other skills at the preset's Skills marker, or its own spot (messages back and a voice). Always sent while on, never dropped.
  6. 6 Try checks a real scene: whether it is on this turn and why, the exact text it adds with macros filled, and its cost.
  7. 7 Skills get their own Library deck with portrait cards: name, when, where linked. New opens on the Workbench.
  8. 8 When the AI opens or closes a skill, the reply shows a small line, like a tool call.
  9. 9 The scene's Skills panel (Props, like Trackers): every linked skill, whether it is on this turn and why, open ones with Close, and the tools note when a picked skill cannot open.
  10. 10 In the prompt it uses the approved placement: the Skills marker in the Legacy list, the Skills node in Nodes.
