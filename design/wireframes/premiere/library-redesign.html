WIREFRAME. The Library in the Premiere house style. Numbered callouts are explained below. The row of buttons under this note is wireframe-only: it flips the room between its full-room states. Everything inside the room is clickable (decks, views, search, staging, import, delete, Trash).

Bunzai · your collection 1

The Library

Every character, world, persona and preset in your studio, shelved by deck. Stage what you need, then send it to the Workbench.

  1. 1 Room header. The IDE-era rail title, the top status line and the Reload Studio button all fold into one Marquee: Cinzel title, a plain-language lede, the primary Add to the library action and the status line (what is shown, studio total). Today these live in three separate strips.
  2. 2 Deck tickets replace the left rail. Same eight decks plus Trash, same counts, same click rule (a ticket shows that deck alone, click it again to show every deck), now as admit-one stubs in deck colors across the top. The count is the big number, so the collection reads at a glance. While a search runs the counts become match counts. Phones stack them two across.
  3. 3 Groups (collections) get a labelled shelf instead of a bar that hides until something is staged. Click a chip to filter (it writes collection:id into the search, as today), Add puts staged pieces in, Remove deletes the group only, New group opens inline naming.
  4. 4 One toolbar panel: search with the full field syntax, All / Recent order, Carousel / Grid layout, art size dial and Select all. The result line sits under it and says whether a shelf is filtered or empty. The dial is a drawn five-step control, not a native slider.
  5. 5 Per-deck view switch: Cards, Tiles, Shelf (Sets for regex, Profiles for personas), Show and List. Each deck remembers its own view, as today. The small script here switches them for real, including the spotlight and the book shelf with Split, Copy and merge.
  6. 6 Each deck is a numbered act: gold numeral, Cinzel name in the deck color, count, its own view switch and its own New button. Empty decks keep their ghost card. A deck with a search match elsewhere says so.
  7. 7 Piece card: portrait cover with the kind flag in the deck color, a drawn check for staging, and an on the workbench stamp in gold. Characters get Start scene on the card. Lorebooks and regex sets carry their on/off switch, Split and Copy. Staged cards take the rose edge.
  8. 8 The staging bar floats at the bottom while pieces are staged: count, Send to the Workbench, Delete, Select all, Clear. Delete opens the Trash confirm (3 days to restore). Drag across empty space sweep-selects, as today.
  9. 9 Damage notice stays a calm panel with a rose edge, not an alarm: count, Dismiss (only in the main room), the reassurance that files were not changed, and the file list with reasons behind a details control.
  10. 10 Trash is a stage of its own: Cinzel title, the 3 day rule, All / Pieces / Scenes filter, Restore per row with days left. The empty Trash has its own state with a way back.
  11. 11 Import sheet: dropped or picked files are read first, then shown as receipts you check off. Shows readable pieces, already-on-shelf and healed notes, identical copies, and grouped refusals. Select all, Add more files, Import N, Not now. Global file drops still work anywhere in the room.
  12. 12 Full-room states keep their own rules: First landing shows two big doors and nothing else; an unreachable studio never offers start fresh, because the pieces are still on disk. Use the wireframe state buttons to see them.
