# Bunraku visual design approvals

This ledger records the accepted designs and requested behavior as approvals continue.
Approval authorizes implementation; it does not claim that a mockup's controls or assets have shipped.
The current authority is the generated black, gold and jewel page set in this folder, together with
the selected crops and corrections in the design conversation.

## Shared appearance

Approved direction: warm black surfaces, gilded gold structure, ivory readable text, and saturated
jewel colors for distinct actions and states. Replace dusty pink and blue-gray chrome throughout
approved surfaces. The Playbill sidebar and its dark paper clouds are approved. The brief requested
the previously designed ticket logo in place of the crossed-ear mark.

## Library

[Library reference](01-library.png): the entire page is approved, including the deck rail, search,
view controls, character cards, shelf headings, and the illustrated lorebook covers. Existing import,
selection, staging, editing, Start Scene, and per-deck tools remain reachable through their owners.
Mockup names, counts, artwork and labels are examples; live UI reads the real library.

Lorebook covers need 60 semantic PNG designs. Covers must support painterly color/background layers,
mixing a design with a color treatment, and a user-authored title. Titles remain editable text.
The painted dark book stock and gilded ornamental designs shown in
the reference were accepted. The collection now contains 60 transparent ornament PNGs and ten painted papers.
The live Library menu opens a cover composer with editable title, layer choices, and a restore-original
action. Choices persist in Studio preferences and leave imported/exported content intact.

## Scenes library

[Scenes reference](02-scenes.png): the Continue your story hero, adjacent transcript preview,
Fan Favorites, and This Week/Earlier recent rows are approved. A choice is wanted between
the existing picture/card view and the approved compact list view. Retain real scene art, time,
take count/pips, active take name, selection, and context menus. The grid remains available.
Favorites represent the person's starred scenes; the illustrative heart counts in the PNG are
not a request for public likes or social statistics.
