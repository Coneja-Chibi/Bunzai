REAL SATURATED JEWEL TONES: deep ruby, emerald, sapphire, amethyst, topaz, aquamarine. Jewel accents must be rich and clear rather than pastel, grayish lavender or dusty pink. Amethyst marks current selection, sapphire marks primary non-destructive action, emerald success/enabled, ruby destructive/error, topaz warning, gold furniture and hierarchy. Use deep jewel fills with ivory text, not pastels with gray text. No pink chrome, no blue-gray panels, no milky gray cards. Useful controls are clear, consistent, aligned and readable: 14–16px labels, 12px minimum small text, appropriately sized click targets, proper fields, tabs, search, menus, scroll areas, explicit state. Quiet serif page/section titles, readable sans body/form text, mono counts and keycaps. Do not add elaborate theater ornaments around every field. Do not turn form pages into story illustrations or poster designs. Theater identity comes from restrained gilded details, dark paper texture, visual hierarchy, and the approved sidebar. Keep substantive app content on screen, realistic density, no enormous blank canvas. Reference sidebar image is aesthetic authority; page-specific instructions below are functional authority. Sample content is allowed ONLY to illustrate the requested existing controls, not to invent features or metrics.
