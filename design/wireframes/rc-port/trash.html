You can restore it from Trash for 3 days. It will no longer appear in your Library or selection menus while deleted.

Ported from RoleCall's Trash (library-client-v2): a Trash row at the foot of the Library rail, the red-edged Trash panel with one row per deleted piece and an accent Restore button, RC's empty state, and RC's "Move to Trash?" dialog. Colors are Bunzai tokens (the danger plate for RC's red). Same as RC: a fixed 3-day window with no setting to change it, and rows show name and type only. Bunzai difference: a restore always comes back under its own name with its history, because a deleted piece's id is never reused. The Library's Delete opens the dialog; the Trash row opens this panel.
