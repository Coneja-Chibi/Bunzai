The idea: scenes on a board in three lanes: On you (they're waiting for your reply), In play (their turn, or just touched), Resting (paused, on purpose or by time). Scenes move lanes on their own as turns pass; drag one to Resting to shelve it without deleting, or back to wake it. Good for people juggling many stories who want order without a spreadsheet.
