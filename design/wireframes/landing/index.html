Ten ways in

Ten landing surfaces for Bunraku as a roleplay app. Each opens on the story, not the library: who is waiting on you, where you left off, and one move to continue or begin.

01
The Puppet Rail
Characters hang from a tilted rail on strings; recent ones hang low and sway, a forgotten one hangs upside down. Your last line waits mid-sentence on a ribbon.
02
The Call Sheet
Tonight's call sheet: every scene a cue with where you left it, a curtain clock, crooked stamps, a scrawled note, cold scenes struck through.
03
Cold Open
No menu. Your last line, enormous, typing itself out and cutting off mid-word; other scenes stick in from the edges as torn strips. Any key continues.
04
The Switchboard
An operator's board: characters are jacks, scenes are sockets, cords droop between them. Lamps flicker where someone is waiting on you; patch a cord to start a scene.
05
The Dressing Room
A split mirror: you (your persona) on one side, who you're opposite tonight on the other, the halves torn out of line and the last line crossing the seam.
06
The Crawl
Bands of your own lines crawl at clashing speeds, angles, and fonts; hover freezes one and names the scene, click drops you in at that line.
07
The Prop Table
Scenes are objects on a table you can shove around: a glowing lamp, a ledger, a tarot card, a dusty rapier. Characters are masks along the edge.
08
The Prompt Book
A marked-up script: stories are acts in script format, a pencil bracket on the one waiting for your line, margin notes writing themselves in.
09
The Contact Sheet
A film contact sheet of scene stills that twitch in the gate; a grease-pencil loop on the one waiting on you, cold scenes glitching until reshot.
10
Ghost Light
A dark stage lit by one bulb; the cursor is a spotlight, characters stand where you left them, and whoever the light finds says their last line to you.
