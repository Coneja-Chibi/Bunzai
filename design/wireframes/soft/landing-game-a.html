Concept A: title screen. The last scene's backdrop fills the stage, drifting slowly with a few floating motes. Press any key (or click) to reveal the vertical menu; arrow keys move the cursor, Enter picks, the mouse works the same. Continue always shows the last scene's name, character, and when. Load scene opens the recent list beside the menu. The Dock is the same one every soft page uses, kept off-canvas for a full-bleed title screen: the handle top-left opens it with one click, or peeks on hover.
