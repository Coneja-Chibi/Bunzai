The big Dock you were just browsing shrinks back into this: the same tiles, the same order, the same colors, at their normal size, fixed to the left on a real page (or, at phone width, as the tab bar below). This placeholder stands in for Scenes itself; see the other soft wireframes for what each app actually shows.

Back to the title screen
Concept A3: the Dock is the title menu. The big menu is the same Dock every soft page uses (workbench-shell.html's own s-app / c-* markup and CSS), just sized up and set under the wordmark, with a Continue tile added on top. The panel on the right previews whatever tile is highlighted. Arrow keys move the highlight and update the preview; Enter opens it. A click selects and opens in one step, like the Dock does everywhere else. Opening hands off to the Dock at its normal size, over a placeholder page, so the shrink from "big menu" to "regular Dock" reads as one continuous idea.
