↑↓ Select ← Dock Hover or arrow onto an app for a preview
Concept A2: Dock beside the title. The Dock stays open on the left at all times, same as the Workbench page. Hovering or arrowing onto any Dock app, or the matching main-menu item, shrinks the wordmark to a small mark up top and turns the stage into a big preview of that app, in its own pastel: Scenes shows the backdrop and Continue card, Workbench its open editors, Library a deck of covers with counts, Press what's staged, Agent the last question, Kit a terminal line, Settings the main switches. Leaving the app returns the preview to whatever the menu has selected.
