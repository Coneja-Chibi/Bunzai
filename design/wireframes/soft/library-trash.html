The Library's Trash, soft identity. Ported from trash-panel.tsx. One list holds deleted pieces, cut scenes, and deleted takes together, filtered by the All / Pieces / Scenes toggle; each row shows its type, its time left ("2d left", "under 1h left" under an hour), and Restore, which brings a piece back under its own id and history, or a scene/take back with every message. Trash is empty is its own state. Move to Trash? is the confirm dialog the Library's Delete opens (one piece or a batch); it is not on this page's own Trash flow, which restores rather than deletes.
