TrackHare, the ported SillyTavern extension, now a real Prop (props/trackhare/hare-stage.tsx, hare-popover.tsx, hare-options.tsx, chains-sheet.tsx, tokens-sheet.tsx). Switch it on per scene in the Props wing (its wing row uses the generic puzzle icon, the deck-regex tone; the carrot mark is TrackHare's own, on the stage button and every sheet header). The button drags anywhere and its corner resizes it (40 to 96px); click opens the popover, right-click or a long press opens options. Popover: Detailed/Compact, grouped by book, reason badges (always on, key, woken by + loop, sticky + turns left, a chance roll on top), Didn't fire with why not, Earlier replies. Tokens: a draggable what-if legend (click to leave a category out, drag to reorder), the budget bar, raw sections with Copy, export. Chains: Starters/Links/Ends/Standalone. Left out, honestly: VectHare (no vector search yet), Potato Mode (nothing to strip), the 18-tokenizer picker (counts with the scene's own model).
