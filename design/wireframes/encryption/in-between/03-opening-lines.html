One real difference from RoleCall: RoleCall has a "Copy to clipboard" button. This version swaps it for "Print the script", because the clipboard is shared with every app and often synced, and on a local app the words are the only master key. If you would rather match RoleCall exactly, the copy button comes back.

Also changed: "memorized my lines" became "written down my lines", since remembering twelve words is not the advice we want to give.
