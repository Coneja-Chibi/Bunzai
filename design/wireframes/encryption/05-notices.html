05 Notices and hard moments

The rare states: a weak lock, a cleanup that could not finish, forgetting a computer, and the moves Bunraku refuses because they would lock the person out. Notices sit at the top of Settings, Security; a small dot on the Settings tile tells the person one is waiting. Nothing pops up over their work.
