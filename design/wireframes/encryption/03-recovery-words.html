03 Recovery words

Twelve words that open the library when everything else is lost: a forgotten password, a new computer, a backup restored elsewhere. Shown once, typed back to prove they were saved, and only then put in force. Offered the first time a password or backups are turned on, and any time from Settings.

Now and then: check you still have them
Do you still have your recovery words?

It has been three months. Type all twelve to check your paper is still right. Bunzai stores no single word, so it cannot check just one or two.

shown as a card in Settings, Security, never as a popup that interrupts work

Why no copy button: the words are a master key; the clipboard is shared with every app and often synced. Printing or writing them keeps them off the computer.
