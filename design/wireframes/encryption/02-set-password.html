02 Set a password

Opened from Settings, Security. A password is optional: without one, this computer's own protection keeps the library safe from a stolen disk. A password adds protection against someone using this computer. Recovery words must exist first, so a forgotten password can never lose the library.

State A: no recovery words yet
First, your recovery words

A password you forget would lock you out for good. Your twelve recovery words are the way back in, so Bunzai makes them first. It takes about a minute.

InkDialog / Stamp / btnGhost

State B: choosing a password, strength shown as you type
Set a password

Weak. Years and common words are the first things guessed. A few unrelated words together are stronger and easier to remember.

When to ask for it
Every time the app opens, and also after you step away if you choose.

meter = MeterBar grown to four segments (zxcvbn score 0 to 4) / when = SettingsRow + SegControl

State C: strong, matching, ready
Set a password

Strong.

After this, every remembered computer forgets its key, and each one will ask for the password. Your recovery words keep working.

State D: changing it later needs a fresh proof
Confirm it is you

Changing your password, words, or remembered computers needs your current password or recovery words. Once confirmed, you will not be asked again for five minutes.

Strength meter: uses zxcvbn (Dropbox's estimator, which you approved), and never blocks: a weak password is allowed after the warning, because the words are the real safety net. The feedback line comes from zxcvbn's own suggestions, rewritten into our voice.
