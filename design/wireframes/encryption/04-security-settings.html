A new section in Settings, beside Connections and Updates, built from the same section cards. One place for the password, the recovery words, and the computers that open the library by themselves.

Why "Protected by Windows": the status names the lock actually used on this computer (Windows protection, macOS Keychain, the Linux keyring, or a key file). A key file is the weak case and gets its own notice (page 05).
