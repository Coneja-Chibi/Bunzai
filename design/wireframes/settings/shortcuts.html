Settings, About: Shortcuts

The setup wizard asks whether to put Bunzai in the Start menu and on the desktop, with Yes picked. A one-time note confirms it after setup. Two switches in Settings, About take them away or put them back.

4. The one-time note, right after the wizard (only when they were added)
Stage direction

Bunzai is now in your Start menu and on your desktop. You can take either away in Settings, About.

Shown once, the first time the studio opens after setup, and only if the shortcuts were added.

Where it shows: only in the Windows desktop app (Bunzai.exe). The browser Studio, the terminal tools, and Mac and Linux show no Shortcuts rows, because nothing there makes shortcuts.

Setup: the setup wizard asks, with Yes picked; a one-time note confirms it. On the browser Studio, Mac and Linux, the wizard has no fifth step.
